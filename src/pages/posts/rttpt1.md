# Part One: _The Unfair and Balanced RTT_

Welcome to part one of my article, where I’m covering the **Unfair and Balanced RTT**.

This one-day tournament takes place near the Swiss border, making it easy for me to attend. The name comes from the two **asymmetric missions** being played. Because of those missions, I might actually have a chance to win a game!

I’m bringing a **character-heavy Adepta Sororitas** list featuring **Morvenn Vahl** and **three Paragon Warsuits** as my primary hammer.  
My secondary damage dealer is a **Palatine with 10 Sacresants**.  
For utility I’ve included a squad of **Seraphim** and a **Mortifier**, and for long-range fire support I have an **Exorcist** and a **Castigator**.

----------

## Game Plan

-   **Versus the meta-dominant “Five Big Knights” lists**  
    I’ll focus one Knight with Vahl’s unit and the other with my fire support and the Palatine squad.  
    The goal is to bring down **1½ to 2 Knights per turn**. Even if I can’t kill one every turn, I still have plenty of scoring pieces.

-   **Versus melee-rush armies**  
    I’ll **screen my units with characters**, then use the revive stratagem to bring them back as needed.


Check back next week for my full **tournament recap**!
